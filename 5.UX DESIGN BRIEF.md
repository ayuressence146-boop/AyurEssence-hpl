UI/UX DESIGN BRIEF — AYURESSENCE
1. Design Vision

AyurEssence is a premium Ayurvedic Prakriti Assessment Platform designed for Doctors, Students, and Patients.

The interface should feel:

Premium
Calm
Professional
Modern
Trustworthy
Ayurveda-inspired
Clean and clinical without looking like a traditional medical application

The design should combine modern healthcare SaaS UI with natural Ayurvedic visual language.
